### Reassessment: This Is Adjunction Usability, With An Unproven Consumer Benefit

The user correctly identifies the candidate as an introduction into the
existing Adjunction relation from whole Hom-comparison data, followed by
computation/agreement of its existing observations. It does not reconstruct
F or G, replace hom_int/homd_int, or make native K/Q/H/δ internal for the
first time. Those functors and transformations already exist.

The earlier [adjunction-usability plan](ADJUNCTION_USABILITY_V3_2_PLAN.md),
sections 5.3–6.2, and its implementation in
`src/v3_2/lf_adjunction_macro.ts` already provide assumeAdjunction and
assumeAdjunctionFromCounitTranspose. Both introduce a trusted instance and
proof-time agreements; they do not infer the adjunction laws from arbitrary
named transformations. The candidate instead requires a whole invertible
natural Hom comparison, including its selected inverse. Such data justify
an ordinary adjunction mathematically, but the present implementation still
adds a structural introduction principle rather than deriving it from the
old primitive interface. This distinction must remain explicit.

The postcomposition reviewer consumes the already declared
one_cat_postcomp_adjunction. It proves interoperability, not a derivation or
replacement of that lift. Source inspection of the terminal/product/family
adjunctions and primary K/Q structures has not identified a production caller
with an independent whole comparison that needs this constructor. In
particular, an intended primary terminal structure may simply take p⊣t as
its universality; routing it through a Hom comparison and back is not a
prerequisite. No dependence of Γ on this constructor has been established.

Promotion is held while this consumer justification is reviewed. The two
candidate modules, reviewers, import/registration changes and their logs
remain uncommitted experiment evidence; no new constructor is claimed as a
qualified library result. UA-1/UA-2 are not complete or removed from the
accepted scope. Next identify the actual input contract and desired output
of a universal construction before adding more general assembly machinery.
Only plan/ledger clarification was performed after this reassessment;
documentation diff/link hygiene is the applicable validation boundary.
