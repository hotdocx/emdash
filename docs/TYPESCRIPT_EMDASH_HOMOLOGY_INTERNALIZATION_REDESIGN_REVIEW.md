## Reading Coverage And What The References Actually Supply

The earlier [Posur/homalg review](TYPESCRIPT_EMDASH_POSUR_HOMALG_REDESIGN_REVIEW.md)
was substantive. The earlier Petrić/van Doorn entries were principally a
reference inventory, not completed design studies. This review additionally
reads the following selected material; it does not claim full book reviews
or reproduction of any external formalization.

- Petrić–Zekić, [2001.09736v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.09736v4):
  introduction, the opening matrix construction in §5, and the proper-object
  restriction and coherence theorem/proof in §7. Their structural language
  has matrices of formal sums of Kelly–Mac Lane graphs. Theorem 7.5 is
  restricted to proper objects in the symmetric-monoidal-closed/biproduct
  case. This can inform structural additive normalization; it is not a
  kernel/cokernel or arbitrary homology normalization theorem.
- Posur, [2103.08379v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.08379v1): revisited
  Theorem 2.2 and Remarks 2.3, 2.10 and 2.12. Universal computation in an
  Adelman category, followed by exact-functor interpretation, is a genuine
  alternative to repeated theorem-by-theorem component chases. Effective
  homotopy solving and the correct integral/universal premise remain
  necessary; rational examples alone are not universal proofs.
- Posur, [1908.04132v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04132v1): revisited
  Theorem 2.18 and Remark 2.19. Generalized morphisms express induced maps
  succinctly. Their pseudo-inverses are not ordinary sections, and a formula
  for a connecting map must not be confused with its exactness proof.
- Barakat–Robertz, [math/0701146v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701146v2):
  revisited the introduction and §1.1. Functors include their morphism
  actions so generic composition and derivation procedures can reuse them.
  Together with the previously reviewed axiomatic homalg paper, this
  strongly supports categorical operation interfaces above interchangeable
  matrix/linear-system algorithms. It does not require canonical remainder
  forms or kernel certification of every native computation.
- Van Doorn, [1808.10690v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.10690v1):
  selected portions of §4.1.1, including Theorem 4.1.1, Definitions 4.1.2–3,
  the five-stage strategy and the sign/index/truncation steps; Chapter 5's
  scope discussion and opening of §5.3. The fiber/loop/truncation route
  is genuinely synthetic. Choosing N × fin3 specifically avoids
  non-definitional endpoint comparisons. Nevertheless the construction
  retains homotopies, exactness arguments and sign coherence. Its exact
  couples are implemented for graded modules, with explicit generality and
  convergence restrictions; it is not a drop-in arbitrary-Abelian-category
  or directed-spectrum calculus.

## Cisinski–Cnossen–Nguyen–Walde: Especially Relevant Sections

The supplied *Synthetic Category Theory* draft is dated September 7, 2026
and has four authors. The [author's project listing](https://cisinski.app.uni-regensburg.de/publikationen.html)
links the evolving book; this review uses the supplied local dated text.
Selected reading covers the introduction/table of contents, §5.9's
functoriality-of-universals principle and pointwise adjunction criterion,
Corollaries 11.9.35–37, Definition 15.1.1, and Definition 15.3.1,
Lemma 15.3.2 and Theorem 15.3.4. The rest is not claimed as reviewed.

The most immediate lead is §5.9, not a jump straight to spectra. Appropriate
cartesian/cocartesian fibrations with fiberwise initial/terminal objects
yield whole adjoint sections. Thus the universal construction itself supplies
functoriality. The hypotheses include an actual fibration, not merely a
list of unrelated objectwise choices. The theorem supplies comparison
isomorphisms; it does not by itself choose emdash runtime rewrite directions.

The nerve/realization results in §11.9 clarify the concrete-constructor
question. A Segal presentation and a complete synthetic category are not
identical raw data; completion/realization has to be accounted for. Chapters
14–15 provide a later route through pointed finite-colimit constructions,
stability and fiber/cofiber sequences. They do not automatically supply a
comparison with our present bounded module homology. This review does not
start stable categories, derived categories or spectra under the current goal.

## A Concrete Simplification Suggested By Posur

For a composable triple a ─α→ b ─β→ c ─γ→ d with γβα = 0, Dowker's formula
expresses the snake connecting map, after the appropriate endpoint
comparisons, as homology applied to the chain map

```text
(a ─α→ b ─γβ→ d)  ─(id, β, id)→  (a ─βα→ c ─γ→ d).
```

This can replace part of a construction; it is not merely a name for the
existing fiber/pushout chase. It does not alone prove all long-exact
statements, and it refers first to the snake connecting map, not directly
to every degree of the homology long-exact sequence. Selected endpoint
comparisons and the sign convention must still be respected.

## Whole Homology Needs More Than A New Functor Name

A promising ordinary-category presentation uses the strict arrow/diagram
category Arr(C), with a zero object 0:

```text
J(X) = (X → 0),      J ⊣ Ker
I(X) = (0 → X),      Coker ⊣ I.
```

These adjunctions follow from the usual annihilator/coannihilator universal
properties. They suggest whole kernel/cokernel functors with actual unit,
counit and mate operations, reusing emdash's adjunction calculus. A lax arrow
category would instead admit a cell where the strict kernel problem asks
for equality to zero; the arrow-category owner must therefore be selected
deliberately. Do not import the unmerged strictness migration implicitly.

On a category of three-term complexes, evaluation gives whole component
functors and differentials as transformations. Kernel yields the cycle
functor, the chain-zero condition induces the boundary transformation,
and cokernel yields homology. Naturality and functoriality can then belong
to generic owners instead of being re-proved in each consumer. Category
and diagram membership should use the internal Hom/fibration machinery,
not new high-level records of manually supplied commuting squares.

Keep choice management explicit: either supply coherent selected operation
providers, or use a category whose objects retain the chosen homology data.
Do not silently replace native-selected kernels or homologies to fit a new
canonical functor. Equality of quotient morphisms also need not mean literal
equality of the underlying matrix representatives or serialized records.

## Concrete Realization: Primitive Presentations Or General Constructors

The user clarified that `make_cat`/`make_func` were schematic ideas, not
required names or a required record-to-category construction architecture.
The codebase already uses primitive category/functor heads with computational
projections and proof-time usability comparisons for concrete instances.
The finite-free and Freyd category declarations are examples. “Concrete”
therefore describes the supplied interpretation/computation, not necessarily
whether the head is defined instead of primitive.

This uniform style is the preferred starting point for the proposed pilot.
Declare the whole owners and connect their object, Hom, component and higher
actions to the actual operations at the appropriate projection positions.
Do not insert a general construction framework merely to obtain a whole
functor. Generic from-data constructors may later remove repeated
realization boilerplate, if a measured consumer needs them.

The active-source audit found `OneCat`/`Struct_ncat` packaging an already
given Cat and dimension evidence, and `path_map_func` constructing a functor
between path categories. These are useful building blocks, but not the
general from-object/Hom/identity/composition/law-record API requested by the
user. No active `make_cat`, `make_func` or `make_transf` family was found;
this absence is not a blocker for the primitive-presentation approach.

If a reusable from-data layer is selected, its inputs would be:

- category data: objects, Hom sets, identity, composition and unit/associativity
  paths;
- functor data: object and arrow maps with identity/composition paths;
- transformation data: components with the appropriate naturality paths.

These concrete records belong at the realization boundary, once. They do
not authorize manual square records throughout the homological theory.
The public names are tentative; a 1-categorical name such as `make_one_cat`
may state the scope more accurately than unrestricted `make_cat`.

The following constraints apply to the chosen realization contract; the
completion issue is particularly relevant to a general from-data constructor:

1. A record of ordinary laws is not arbitrary omega-category data. Our
   current `PreadditiveCategory` constrains `Hom C x y = Obj(Hom_cat C x y)`;
   it does not alone make every directed higher Hom discrete. Concrete
   finite-free/Freyd models use path categories of matrix/quotient sets,
   but that fact must not silently be assumed for every abstract C.
2. If the target is complete/univalent, raw object records may require a
   realization/completion. Do not promise that Obj(make_cat D) is literally
   D.objects without auditing the target classifier. An object-introduction
   map and correct Hom comparisons may be the right interface.
3. Supplied law paths are not automatically judgmental equations for arbitrary
   field algorithms. Keep generic category/functor computation as owner,
   connect concrete implementations through justified computation/usability
   interfaces, and probe the actual identity/composition/naturality consumers.
   The earlier finite-free category's raw-composition experiments are relevant
   evidence, not a reason to repeat an unchecked broad rewrite.

## What Can Disappear, And What Cannot Be Assumed Away

Much of today's endpoint/projection bookkeeping is representational and can
be avoided. Whole functors and universal transformations can centralize
identity, composition, naturality and reconstruction laws. Existing path
proofs are useful semantic evidence for installing appropriate computational
owners, not necessarily the final user-facing form.

Substantive exactness and compatibility facts still need a mathematical
justification, a stated trusted contract or a universal interpretation.
Native computation still needs actual arithmetic, membership, syzygy and
factor algorithms. Neither requires keeping every current combinatorial
calculation or replaying every arithmetic step in the kernel. Generalized
morphisms, universal Abelian diagrams, and eventually fiber/cofiber methods
can move that work into reusable constructions.
