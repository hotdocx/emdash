## Independent Review

The frozen proposal consolidates two TypeScript presentation paths around an
already-reviewed recursive displayed compiler. It adds no mathematical owner
and does not equate fixed categories with open fibres.

`compileDisplayedContextual` is the correct existing semantic boundary. Its
one-binding wiring is the displayed identity on the source family; therefore
its result is a displayed functor from exactly that source family, not a
total-context encoding or a cast. The audit's four byte-identical
identity/eta/composition/weakening comparisons establish a strong rollback
oracle for the historical compact factorer.

The profile gate is necessary and sufficient. `fibred-binder-1` does not
enable displayed contextual abstraction, so its frozen three-body contract
continues to reject non-chain syntax. Later contextual profiles already own
fibre product pairing, fixed/varying displayed evaluation, their object and
arrow reductions, and the requisite Sigma/Pi bridge. Reaching those operations
from compact or expanded notation is therefore frontend coverage, not a new
kernel capability.

The ordering of the paths is sound: qualified section weakening remains first;
direct identity/eta/closed chains retain their old evidence rules, chain
lengths, recovered terms, and exact Core; only an otherwise-unmatched body in
a contextual-enabled profile reaches the recursive engine. The new
`categorical.displayed-functor-contextual` evidence rule accurately records
that third route without changing the resulting Core classifier.

Explicit nonlocal-usage rejection is required even though the recursive
compiler already rejects many malformed shapes. It makes the compact and
expanded no-capture boundary independent of incidental body cases and prevents
a later contextual operation from silently broadening scope.

Propagating structural prerequisites is also required. Fibre pairing and
displayed evaluation introduce reviewed product/evaluation structure that the
narrow chain factorer never needed to report. Dropping that metadata would
make the surface evidence incomplete even if the Core happened to check.

The selected positive consumers are representative and non-ad-hoc. The fixed
evaluation `lambda^f F. F(a0)` exercises recursive application at the inner
position requested by the end-user usability programme; the one-variable
fibre diagonal exercises repeated variable occurrence and displayed
contraction. Both already pass through `displayedContextLambda`, so exact
compact/contextual/expanded parity tests compare three presentations of one
existing internal construction. Consuming the displayed full action at a base
arrow verifies that the change does not reduce the feature to object-only
syntax.

The proposal correctly leaves text, browser, transfer, historical contracts,
and Lambdapi untouched. Those layers may consume the richer typed behavior in
later separately reviewed slices, but they are not prerequisites for this
compiler consolidation.

## Decision

Approve exactly `DISPLAYED-FUNCTOR-CONTEXTUAL-ENGINE-1F` as frozen at the
reviewed checkpoint. Implement only the ten numbered items. Preserve the
historical fast paths and base-profile rejection, require exact
compact/contextual/expanded Core parity, propagate both prerequisite classes,
and reject nonlocal capture. Do not add a universal fixed/displayed compiler,
new kernel/Core semantics, a public method, text/browser behavior, or transfer
work in this tranche.
