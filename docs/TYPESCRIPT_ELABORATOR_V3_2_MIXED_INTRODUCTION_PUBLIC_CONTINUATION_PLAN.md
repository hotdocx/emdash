## Objective

Advance the completed recursive-mixed architecture in two deliberately
different stages:

1. expose the already-checked qualified recursive Hom capability honestly in
   the existing public browser reviewer and README; then
2. make variance-correct mixed curry/introduction the next substantive
   mathematical and TypeScript usability objective.

The first stage is product synchronization, not new mathematics. The second
stage is new mathematical capability and must begin Lambdapi-first.

The plan must keep these distinctions explicit:

- finite recursive *formation and elimination* of qualified nested Hom
  categories are implemented;
- general mixed *introduction* of a coherent term is not implemented;
- meaningful variance is typed and polarity-constrained, not an unrestricted
  syntactic permission;
- `Product_catd` facade consistency is a maintainability question, not a
  prerequisite of the likely two-sided mixed-curry domain; and
- publication of existing evidence must not be confused with semantic
  graduation of future mixed curry.

## Consolidated Priority Assessment

### 1. Synchronize the existing public reviewer first

The browser is already deployed at
`https://hotdocx.github.io/emdash/`. Promotion therefore means exposing a
newly completed checked capability through the existing reviewer—not building
another frontend, backend, checker, or deployment architecture.

The current public README still says that displayed-context “arbitrary
depth” is open. That is now too coarse. D-DTTLF-USABILITY-032 established
arbitrary finite Hom depth for one exact qualified canonical grammar, and
D-DTTLF-USABILITY-033 exposed its category formation through the recursive
text adapter. The public wording should distinguish:

- implemented depth-generic finite Hom/category recursion over supported
  canonical roots; from
- still-open arbitrary variance, arbitrary displayed-former introduction,
  exchange across dependency, mixed curry, and unrestricted coherence.

This is a high-confidence, user-facing tranche. It gives the completed
semantic work an inspectable end-user expression and prevents the public
boundary from becoming stale.

### 2. Make qualified mixed introduction the next research objective

The next genuine usability limitation is not type formation. Emdash can form
and eliminate nested `Hom_catd`/`Functor_catd`/`Transf_catd` structures in its
qualified grammar. It cannot yet introduce an arbitrary internally coherent
mixed term from a convenient contextual body.

For example, exact eta of an already coherent term is implemented, while a
body that genuinely constructs and evaluates a mixed functor requires a
variance-sensitive curry/evaluation owner. A TypeScript whole-body pattern
matcher must not fabricate that mathematics.

The substantive continuation should therefore combine:

- an exact two-sided context and polarity audit;
- one explicit mixed-curry functor;
- internal object and base-arrow action;
- generic Hom action for transformations and higher cells where the existing
  kernel exposes it;
- TypeScript transfer through the generic declaration/runtime/checker path;
- one genuinely non-eta mixed contextual abstraction; and
- a final exact variance/introduction graduation boundary.

This is likely feasible, but it is not mechanical in the same sense as the
public browser route. Its exact owner signature, variance, computation, and
laws require mathematical design and bounded Lambdapi probes.

### 3. Do not make “unrestricted variance” the immediate target

Meaningful variance is necessarily typed. A covariant displayed family, a
contravariant source family, and a two-sided mixed context do not admit every
syntactic permutation. The scalable goal is arbitrary finite composition of
supported positive, negative, and mixed constructors under checked polarity,
not acceptance of every alternation.

Accordingly the plan uses **qualified mixed introduction** and **staged
variance coverage**. Each new grammar edge must correspond to an internally
owned categorical construction and must fail closed when no such
factorization exists.

### 4. Keep the product facade conditional

The active kernel represents the fibrewise product of two same-base displayed
families by a transparent composite and gives stable names to its projections,
pairing, swap, diagonal, and action owners. Earlier probes rejected an
additional injective `Product_catd` head as duplicated semantics with warning
overlap.

A consistently adopted transparent alias may still improve source
readability, diagnostics, and transfer ergonomics. But the plausible mixed
curry uses a two-sided Sigma/pullback context because its source and target
families have different polarities. A same-base `Product_catd` facade is
therefore not presently a mathematical dependency.

`PRODUCT-FACADE-0A` becomes active only if the mixed-introduction audit shows
that repeated transparent composites materially obstruct the exact owner
signature or transfer. Even then it starts as a read-only comparison among:

1. the present repeated composite;
2. one consistently adopted transparent alias; and
3. a stable primitive head.

No kernel alias or primitive follows merely from stylistic uniformity.

### 5. Treat ordinary curry-package coherence as supporting evidence

Both ordinary `curry_func_func` and `uncurry_func_func` are transparent
defined packages. Their being simultaneously defined is not itself a defect.
The relevant questions are whether object, hom, transfor, and next-hom action
are internally exposed; whether beta/eta/naturality/adjunction laws exist at
the correct level; and whether transparent unfolding remains manageable.

`CURRY-PACKAGE-0A` is therefore a supporting sub-audit inside
`MIXED-INTRODUCTION-0A`. It becomes an independent implementation row only if
the mixed probe finds a concrete missing action, law, or performance failure.

## Browser Preset Design

The initially approved public witness would have used the already-qualified
`fibred-transfd-1` profile and two recursive Hom levels over a
displayed-transformation category.

Host fixture:

```text
K : Cat
E,D : Catd K
F0,F1 : Functord E D
eta,theta : Transfd F0 F1
alpha,beta : Hom_(Transfd F0 F1)(eta,theta)
```

Editable source:

```text
id (hom (hom (transfd F0 F1) eta theta) alpha beta)
```

Direct TypeScript counterpart:

```text
const transformations = displayedTransforCategory(F0,F1)
const cells = homCategory(transformations,eta,theta)
const higherCells = homCategory(cells,alpha,beta)
identityFunctor(higherCells)
```

This expression would have demonstrated all of the intended public claim:

1. `transfd F0 F1` reaches the existing internalized displayed-transformation
   category;
2. the first `hom` forms its cell category;
3. the second `hom` recursively forms the next higher-cell category;
4. `id` consumes the recursively constructed category as a normal category
   operand; and
5. the resulting term compiles through the same explicit Core and generic LF
   checker as direct TypeScript.

Focused implementation disproved the fixture-profile premise:
`displayedTransforCategory` is guarded by
`fibred-displayed-nd-higher-1`, while `fibred-transfd-1` fails closed with
`UNAVAILABLE_DISPLAYED_ND_HIGHER`. The semantically valid higher profile took
72.67 seconds cold on the current host, which is inappropriate for an
interactive browser preset. No profile weakening or local conversion is
justified.

The corrected public witness therefore uses the default profile and the
already checked category expression:

```text
id (hom (hom (functor A B) F G) alpha beta)
```

Here `F` and `G` are objects of `Functor_cat A B`, while `alpha` and `beta`
are objects of `Hom_cat (Functor_cat A B) F G`. This still exercises the same
depth-generic recursive `hom` resolver twice, with a nontrivial
functor-category root. Its exact direct TypeScript compilation succeeds in
under fifteen seconds cold including process/module initialization on the
current host. It deliberately does not advertise the stronger displayed
Transfd root in the browser; that root remains covered by completed semantic
TypeScript evidence under its qualified profile.

It deliberately avoids:

- the slow `fibred-displayed-mixed-nest-1` initialization;
- a browser-only category-result renderer;
- a second expected-result contract;
- new binder or introduction semantics; and
- any claim that two visible levels are an algorithmic depth limit.
