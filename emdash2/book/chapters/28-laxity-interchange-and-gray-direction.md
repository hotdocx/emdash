# 28. Laxity, Interchange, And The Gray Direction

Functoriality is often printed as an equation. In a directed higher setting,
the more informative object is the comparison cell that the equation would
erase. Its source is the composite of the two separate arrow actions; its
target is the action on the composite arrow. Retaining that cell is the first

projects them pointwise. Displayed action yields the fibrewise comparison;
ordinary post/left and pre/right surfaces are then recovered from the same
owner. The usual functor compositor is the identity-transformation
specialization, not an unrelated square axiom. Reapplying the hom action
retains the next-dimensional coherence instead of ending at one component.

To test this mechanism geometrically, choose the walking arrow $I$ and the
selected right-closed Gray profile. Its internal hom has computationally
strict functors as objects while its arrows reuse the ambient transformation

## 28.1 A Profile, Not A Duplicate Theory

Strict and lax maps share most of their mathematical infrastructure. Rather
than duplicate products, transformations, modifications, and every higher
hom, the selected Gray hom uses a strict-object profile inside the ambient
lax-capable calculus. A stable decoder marks the functors whose compositor
computes to identity at the profile owner. The surrounding transformations
and higher cells remain those of the common functor category.

This design also makes the limitation precise. The current construction has
one right closure, its curry/uncurry mapping equivalence, coevaluation and
evaluation at selected strict codes, and the walking-square interchanger. It
does not yet supply the mirror closure, tensor action in every parameter,
associativity and unit data, or a proof of the full Crans-Gray biclosed
monoidal structure. Historical global strict endpoint cuts also remain a
prototype simplification; the explicit interchanger cell itself is not
reduced to identity.

The fourth spiral therefore ends where the next research programme begins.
Groupoidal realization shows how directed coherence becomes invertible.
Gray-shaped structure shows why the directed coherence was worth retaining in
the first place.
