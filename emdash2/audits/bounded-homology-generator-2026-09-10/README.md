## What Is Qualified

- Zero identity transfers backwards through monomorphisms and forwards
  through epimorphisms. Applying this to the retained cycles and quotient
  gives zero identity at the SAME homology object when the middle term is
  zero. The whole-H specialization and terminal-middle case retain literal
  H endpoints; no object cast or global runtime identity collapse is added.
- The generic window extension prepends the three original exact pairs and
  their proofs to an exact continuation. Empty continuations and the prior
  two-window result are concrete consumers.
- A coherent three-row state and finite row span retain actual whole row
  functors and transformations. The remainder is indexed by the shifted
  original state. Existing four-row windows are derived from it. Whole
  `tapp1_func` comparisons and the wrong-remainder negative preserve the
  higher-action and shared-input boundaries.

The four zero/extension modules introduce definitions and proofs only. The
two row-input record modules have ten local constructor/projection beta
rules; there is no new unifier or generic cut. Warning comparison preserves
every inventory field at 1,249 critical-pair and 169 pattern reports.

probe paths. [manifest.json](manifest.json) records their original paths,

mathematical counterexample. The corrected source and coherent span checks
pass. No source type/rule was weakened to repair that call.

The owning source/reviewer gate is
`scripts/check_homology_bounded_prerequisites.sh`. It checks the exact
dependency join in a fresh temporary copy with at most 90 seconds per
source or reviewer. The actual Nat generator, zero-padding/degree order,
endpoint application, formal-boundary integration and final book work are
still required; this prerequisite checkpoint does not complete the goal.
