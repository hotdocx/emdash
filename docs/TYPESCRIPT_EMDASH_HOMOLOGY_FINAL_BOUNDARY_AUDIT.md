## What The Proof-CAS Connection Actually Does

The [whole replay/adoption workflow](../src/v3_2/algebra_formal_freyd_long_exact.ts)
replays one categorical program and reconstructs its equation inventory from
the actual returned result. The
[selected-interior workflow](../src/v3_2/algebra_formal_freyd_long_exact_homology.ts)
then reuses the selected weak-pullback providers, raw boundary and
reconstruction to construct formal homology and exactness for every displayed
interior pair. It does not turn boundary epicity alone into kernel universality.

The [whole-model workflow](https://github.com/hotdocx/emdash/blob/df9b4778584121c3ad6ef7575093bd6d84be89e8/src/v3_2/algebra_formal_freyd_long_exact_model.ts)
adds an interpretation of actual whole-H observations. It checks the issued
preparation, original adoption, exact equation inventory, model signature and
named supplied model before recording further claims. Its three evidence
classes are deliberately different:

1. **Computed equations** supply particular relation-preservation and raw
   agreement witnesses over the fixed represented coefficient ring.
2. **Selected-provider semantics** supply all-test factor operations at the
   original weak-pullback/kernel choices. A finite set of equations is not
   their universal proof.
3. **Whole-model presentation semantics** interpret formal H points, induced
   maps, model-side short-exact rows and connecting observations at the
   retained native objects/arrows. Normality remains a typed supplied input.

The implementation reports `connectingCoverage = not-requested` without
normality; it does not silently return a supposedly complete model sequence.
With normality, the returned displayed-order view is checked against EVERY
original native arrow. Existing assumptions are reused by exact claim type,
and all adoption remains explicit and append-only.

The [bounded consumer](../tests/v3_2_algebra_formal_freyd_long_exact_homology_tests.ts)
checks 18 H observations, eight induced maps and three connecting windows in
its nonsplit two-degree fixture. Its seven displayed arrows are connecting,
inclusion, projection, connecting, inclusion, projection, connecting; only
the middle connecting is nonzero. It verifies original object/arrow identity,
shared row proofs, preserved prefix assumptions and typed formal observations.
Spies prohibit homology, connecting, window and weak-universal reselection
during interpretation. Invalid inventories, model inputs, normality and
changed endpoint/selection data are rejected before further trust.

The carried-forward `09f9dd1a` qualification includes the concrete opt-in
Lambdapi targets for all three connecting observations, their nine raw row
maps and explicit interpretation assumptions. The source equality check in
this audit confirms that this workflow and consumer have not changed. The
multi-minute unchanged conformance suite was not rerun for book-only edits.

## Computational And Logical Boundaries

- Whole H is derived from K/Q and a whole boundary transformation. The native
  source is a one-degree zero-diagram category under the ordinary-target
  profile, not a newly asserted category of all unbounded complexes.
- Connecting has an actual whole transfor with its direct component
  computation. Its caller supplies coherent row functors/maps, not a new
  collection of manually written naturality squares.
- The selected record/universal-factor APIs remain useful consumers of the
  same choices. Existing raw wrappers are not deleted merely to make the
  presentation look uniform; native/model inputs still use them.
- Equality of serialized data is not Freyd quotient equality. No arbitrary
  truncated quotient path is decoded into a raw matrix witness.
- The provider is interpreted over its original coefficient ring. Presented
  modules over that ring are supported; silently carrying kernel choices
  across a nonzero coefficient-ring quotient is not.
- The conventional zero-ended symbolic theorem is not inferred from native
  endpoint tests or the generic window theorem. Its final attachment remains
  user-deferred, as do the unpromoted indexed-interface/checker experiments.
- Primitive-projection record structures remain a later design option.
  General homological normalization, closed model construction, unbounded or
  derived categories and spectral sequences remain separate work.
- The inherited whole-opposite/Sigma-Hom variance defects remain open. Current
  typechecking is not a consistency or semantic-soundness certificate. No
  invalid reversal is adopted as a construction in this tranche.
