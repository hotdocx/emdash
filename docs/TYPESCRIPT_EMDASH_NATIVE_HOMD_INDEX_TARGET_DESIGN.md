## Decision From The New Counterexample

The old local target cannot be repaired for arbitrary directed bases merely
by changing the y-base duality or replacing positive sections by negative
ones. Its fibre and base-arrow formulas already fail together on a strict
walking 2-cell, with ordinary finite fibre categories and strict displayed
maps. This is a mathematical obstruction to that supporting expression,
independent of the known Lambdapi inconsistencies or checker behavior.

Retain hom_int/homd_int as native syntactic owners. Investigate a supporting
target whose objects keep the whole shared triangular index, rather than
first declaring the unrestricted y-local functor category to be a directed
family. The checked finite construction below is a proposed semantic
interpretation of that supporting target. It is not a replacement definition
of dependent Hom by total-category Hom, nor a new end-user cone record API.

## A Strict Finite Counterexample

Let Z have objects x,y, parallel arrows p,q:x→y and one nonidentity
2-cell α:p⇒q. All other nonidentity cells and compositions are absent.
Let I=[1]. Define the strict 2-functor D by

```text
Dₓ=1,       Dᵧ=I,
D(p)(*)=0,  D(q)(*)=1,  D(α)=the arrow 0→1.
```

The old section target at the fixed source x has fibres

```text
Tₓ ≃ Cat,
Tᵧ = Fun(I, Fun(Iᵒᵖ, Cat)).
```

Its required restriction along p evaluates H at (0,p), while restriction
along q evaluates H at (1,q). A whole base-2-cell action in one orientation
would require functors H(0,p)→H(1,q), natural in every H; the other
orientation requires H(1,q)→H(0,p).

These two points are incomparable in I×Iᵒᵖ. In particular, there are strict
functors H with the following values. Rows are v=0,1; columns are p,q;
0 means the empty category and 1 the terminal category.

| Model | H(0,p) | H(0,q) | H(1,p) | H(1,q) | Impossible requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | H(0,p)→H(1,q) |
| B | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | H(1,q)→H(0,p) |

Both requirements would supply a functor 1→0. Changing the lax/oplax
naturality convention does not repair this component obstruction: such a
transformation still needs its component functor at H. Choosing the other
orientation of α merely exchanges the two examples.

These are also actual native dependent-Hom inputs, not just arbitrary
unrealizable objects of the old target. Put Eₓ=the discrete category {0,1},
Fₓ(*)=1, E(p)=(a,b), E(q)=(c,d), and Fᵧ=(b,d). Take u=0. Define Eᵧ to be
the finite poset generated by the indicated relations:

| Model | Generating order relations in Eᵧ |
| --- | --- |
| A | a→b, b→d, a→c |
| B | a→c, c→d, b→d |

In both cases a→c and b→d define E(α); Fᵧ is a functor, and F:D⇒E is
strictly natural on p,q and α. The original endpoint formula gives exactly
the displayed table:

```text
H(v,r) = HomEᵧ(E(r)(u), Fᵧ(v)).
```

Thus restricting the local target to values produced by the native Hom
formula does not remove these examples. The problem is making that local
fibre formula into the old y-indexed family before recording the shared
triangular compatibility.

## Proposed Supporting Index In The Strict 2-Dimensional Profile

For a strict 2-functor D:Z→Cat, with ordinary categories Dᵧ, define the
reference index Sₓ(D) as follows:

```text
object:       (y,v,a:x→y), where v∈Dᵧ;
arrow:        (s,β,θ):(y,v,a)→(z,w,b),
              s:y→z, β:D(s)(v)→w, θ:b⇒s∘a;
2-cell γ:     (s,βₛ,θₛ)⇒(t,βₜ,θₜ),
              γ:s⇒t,
              βₛ = βₜ∘D(γ)ᵥ,
              θₜ = (γ∗a)∘θₛ.
```

Composition is the inherited pasting:

```text
(t,δ,φ)∘(s,β,θ) = (t∘s, δ∘D(t)(β), (t∗θ)∘φ).
```

This reference definition explains a native directed context extension.
The eventual formal interface should internalize its compatibility through
the native owners, not ask the caller to manipulate these records manually.
For general higher fibres, equalities in this reference description must
be replaced by the correctly oriented higher cells and their coherence;
the ordinary-fibre model does not specify that extension automatically.

For strict E and F:D⇒E, the source-fixed native Hom observation is a whole
functor Mₓ,u on this index, with

```text
Mₓ,u(y,v,a) = HomEᵧ(E(a)(u), Fᵧ(v)),
Mₓ,u(s,β,θ)(h) = F_z(β)∘E(s)(h)∘E(θ)ᵤ.
```

The formula uses strict E-composition and strict F-naturality to identify
the intermediate endpoints. The two 2-cell conditions give equality of
the resulting functions on Hom sets. For a lax F with the existing direction
E(s)Fᵧ⇒F_zD(s), its comparison component belongs between F_z(β) and
E(s)(h); qualification must derive this observation from the native
internal action. For a lax E, an inverse compositor cannot be assumed.
That profile needs a further construction or an explicit appropriate
coherence capability, not a silent extension of this formula.

The y-local projection is recovered by restricting Mₓ,u to objects with
fixed y and to their v/a action. It remains the original homd_tgt_func and
homd_ observation. Its existence does not assert that all such local
functors form the old y-family.

## Joint Source Variance

For r:x→x′, precomposition gives S(r):Sₓ′(D)→Sₓ(D), sending a to a∘r.
For ρ:r⇒r′, there is a transformation S(r′)⇒S(r): its component uses
the triangle a∘r⇒a∘r′. This retains the noninvertible cell; it is not
an equality of the two source-change functors.

At this profile and dimension, S is contravariant in both source arrows
and source 2-cells. Applying the strict functor-category construction gives
the following candidate whole target, with the appropriate universe levels:

```text
P_D(x) = Fun(Sₓ(D), Cat),
P_D : R(Z) → Cat,
homd_int(F) : Eᴼ ⇒ P_D over R(Z).
```

Here R reverses dimensions from 2 upwards and O reverses all positive
dimensions. In this strict 2-dimensional model only their dimensions 1/2
are tested. The displayed whole signature is a design target, not a checked
new LP declaration or proof of the ω-dimensional extrapolation.

| Variable or action | Required reading in the reference model | Native owner to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| u in Eₓ | contravariant: precompose E(a)(u′→u) | homd_src_func |
| v in Dᵧ | covariant: postcompose Fᵧ(v→v′) | homd_tgt_func |
| a in HomZ(x,y) | contravariant: precompose E(a⇒a′) | homd_ |
| target y change | shared index arrow (s,β,θ), with its actual compatibility | homd_src_sec and its whole action; revise its classifier |
| source r:x→x′ | P_D(r) is restriction along S(r) | homd_int |
| source ρ:r⇒r′ | P_D reverses the 2-cell; Eᴼ has the same base variance | native internal Hom action and higher projections |
| general D input | D remains over Z; Sₓ(D) retains its original action | no D↦D′ over R(Z) premise |

Variation of the family input also has a concrete reference direction. For
a lax map G:D⇒D′ with comparison D′(s)Gᵧ⇒G_zD(s), the candidate
Sₓ(G):Sₓ(D)→Sₓ(D′) sends v to Gᵧ(v) and β to
G_z(β)∘G_s(v), preserving the same triangle θ. Precomposition then
acts contravariantly on P. Its full higher profile and the corresponding
whole constructor must be qualified; the finite audit below fixes D and
does not test this extra map constructor.

For the internal action associated to F:E⇒D, the fixed-source component
has its existing intended reading

```text
h ↦ Fᵧ(h)∘F_p(u),
F_p(u):D(p)(Fₓ(u))→Fᵧ(E(p)(u)).
```

This uses the paired source action Fₓ and index action Sₓ(F). It does not
justify the old unrestricted Op_funcd(F) in the same lax-map category:
duality reverses its base comparison. The whole tdapp1_int/fdapp1_int
classifier must retain the resulting mixed profile or express this module
action directly. Preserving its original primitive ownership is compatible
with correcting that classifier. Do not silently infer the old whole type
from the valid fixed-source formula.

This resolves the old input-base mismatch at the level of the proposed
strict 2-dimensional target expression: D is an input to Sₓ(D), rather
than being incorrectly inserted into a mixed-family constructor over
R(Z). It does not establish that the higher-dimensional constructor exists
with every current generic profile.

## Profiles And Foundational Ownership

Keep distinct the external action of dimension-selected dualities on strict
ω-categories/functors and their action on internal functor categories.
For a transformation with comparison G(f)∘ηₓ⇒ηᵧ∘F(f), applying homwise
duality R reverses that comparison while retaining the component direction.
It therefore cannot be declared a same-profile action on arbitrary lax
transformations merely from its object formula. The existing preferred
prefix's CoAbove2_functor_cat_func still needs that qualification.

The strict/Gray distinction and total-duality compatibility used by the
parent review are supported by Ara–Guetta,
[§§2.22–2.27](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.08832v3).
The finite counterexample and Sₓ(D) calculations here are our own explicit
derivations. They do not claim that this paper provides an emdash kernel
implementation or a ready-made formula for every dimension-selected profile.

Implementation dependency remains:

```text
native hom_int / homd_int with a qualified supporting target
  → native section/internal-action projections
  → extracted displayed laxity and Sigma action.
```

Do not define the supporting target through a Sigma/comma operation whose
implementation already requires this same dependent-Hom action. A semantic
comma interpretation does not itself solve that dependency. The next owner
review must identify a native context/index construction with independent
ownership, or justify a supporting primitive by a complete higher action
and noncircular computation account. A fresh opaque symbol plus point
projections would be insufficient.
